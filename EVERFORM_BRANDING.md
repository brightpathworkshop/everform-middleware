## 2. Voice & Tone

### Core principles
1. **Direct.** No padding, no over-explaining, no apology tours.
2. **Human.** Sounds like a real person at a small company, not a corporate support bot.
3. **Skimmable with depth.** Bold hooks for skimmers, substance underneath for readers.
4. **Confident without being preachy.** We know what we sell and why it's better. We don't lecture.
5. **Benefit-forward.** When explaining a constraint or process, lead with what the customer gets, not what we can't do.

### Writing rules

**Always:**
- Use contractions (I'm, you'll, we're, doesn't)
- Keep sentences short
- Lead with the point, then justify it
- Use active voice
- Use plain words when they exist

**Never:**
- Use em dashes (—). Use hyphens, periods, or restructure the sentence. This is a hard rule. Em dashes read as AI-generated.
- Use semicolons in customer-facing copy
- Use "I apologize for any inconvenience," "please do not hesitate," or other corporate boilerplate
- Use emojis unless the customer used one first
- Pad responses with filler ("Great question!" "I hope this helps!")
- Quote pricing, stock levels, or commission rates in chat or written copy. Direct people to the site.
- Reveal sourcing, lab partner relationships, or operational details
- Use the words "cutting-edge," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "best-in-class," or similar marketing clichés
- Reference specific dosing, protocols, or human use

### Tone calibration by surface

| Surface | Tone |
|---|---|
| Homepage / landing pages | Confident, clinical, minimal. Three pillars over paragraphs. |
| Product pages | Factual. What it is, what's tested, what's in the package. |
| Policy pages | Clear and plain. No legalese theater. |
| SMS to members | Short, casual, direct. Like a text from a coworker. |
| Email to members | Slightly more structured than SMS but still concise. |
| AI chat agent | Helpful, transparent (signs as "Everform Life," not a fake person). |
| Application / onboarding | Warm but measured. Doesn't oversell access. |

### Good vs. bad examples

**Good:**
> Hey, just checking in. Did you get the agreement email? Sometimes it lands in spam.

**Bad:**
> Hello! I wanted to follow up regarding the Research Use Agreement that was sent to your email address. Please check your spam folder if you have not received it. Let me know if you have any questions.

**Good:**
> You'll get a payment link by text and email within about a minute. Returning members with a card on file are charged automatically.

**Bad:**
> Due to the unique nature of our payment processing system, customers will receive an invoice via Square instead of paying at checkout. This is to ensure compliance and security.

## 3. Visual Identity

### Logo

**Marks available:**
- Horizontal logo (`EverformLifeLogoHorizontal.png`) — primary use for site headers, email signatures, wide layouts
- Vertical logo (`EverformLifeLogoVertical.png`) — use for square/social, packing inserts, favicon variants
- Black-only emblem — use for thermal-printed vial labels (Niimbot B1) and any single-color application

**Construction:**
- Hexagonal "E" emblem inside a circle, ringed in Brushed Gold on Midnight Navy
- Wordmark: "EVERFORM" in navy, dot separator in gold, "LIFE" in gold
- The dot between EVERFORM and LIFE is a brand element. Don't drop it.

**Clear space:** Maintain space equal to the height of the "E" emblem on all sides of the logo.

**Don't:**
- Don't recolor the emblem outside of full-color, all-navy, or all-black
- Don't stretch, rotate, or add effects (drop shadows, glows, gradients)
- Don't place on busy backgrounds or low-contrast colors
- Don't use the wordmark without the emblem in primary brand placements

## 4. Color Palette

### Primary

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight Navy | `#0B132B` | Primary text, headlines, button backgrounds, emblem field |
| Brushed Gold | `#C5A059` | Accents, dot separator, hover states, "premium" highlights |
| Clinical White | `#FFFFFF` | Page background, button text on navy |

### Supporting

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Slate Gray | `#4A5568` | Body copy, secondary text |
| Verified Teal | `#2D6A4F` | "In Stock," "Verified," "Tested" badges |
| Warning Red | `#9B2C2C` | "Research Use Only" notices, error states. Use sparingly. |

### Application rules

- **Background:** Always pure white. No off-whites, no cream, no dark mode for primary surfaces.
- **Headlines:** Midnight Navy. Always.
- **Body copy:** Slate Gray for blocks of text. Midnight Navy for short emphasis.
- **Buttons:** Navy background, white text. Hover: lighten navy or shift to gold.
- **Navigation:** White background, navy text. Minimalist apothecary feel.
- **Gold:** Use as a finishing touch, not a foundation. It's the accent on a clinical canvas.

## 5. Typography

### Fonts

| Use | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines (display, marketing pages) | **Cormorant Garamond** | Serif. Adds the "apothecary" weight. |
| Body, UI, navigation | **DM Sans** | Sans-serif. Clean, modern, readable. |
| Vial labels & thermal printing | **Inter** or **Helvetica** | Sans-serif. High contrast for Niimbot B1 thermal output. |

### Hierarchy guidelines

- H1: Cormorant Garamond, 48-64px desktop, 32-40px mobile, weight 500-600
- H2: Cormorant Garamond, 32-40px desktop, weight 500
- H3: DM Sans, 20-24px, weight 600
- Body: DM Sans, 16-18px, weight 400, line height 1.6
- Caption / small print: DM Sans, 13-14px, weight 400

Don't mix more than these two faces in customer-facing layouts.

## 6. The Three Pillars (Core Differentiators)

These are the three claims that anchor every piece of marketing copy. Lead with them.

1. **Independent ISO/IEC 17025 Testing**
   Every batch tested at an A2LA-accredited lab (Vanguard Scientific Solutions). Full COAs published with raw chromatograms and mass spec traces, not summary reports.

2. **Cold-Chain Integrity**
   Stored cold and shipped cold. Phase-change material, foam-walled shippers, Monday-Wednesday shipping windows during summer.

3. **Members-Only Accountability**
   Vetted application process. We know who we're working with. No anonymous orders.

When writing landing pages, product pages, or pitches: these three pillars are the spine.

## 7. Page & Component Patterns

### Page structure

- White background, generous whitespace
- Headline (Cormorant Garamond, navy)
- Short subhead (DM Sans, slate)
- Three-pillar layout where applicable
- Clear single CTA per section (navy button, white text)
- No sliders, no carousels, no auto-playing video

### Shopify policy pages
- Use rich text only, no HTML
- Plain headings, plain paragraphs
- No injected CSS or scripts

### HTML pages (custom)
- Use the brand palette exclusively
- Cormorant Garamond + DM Sans
- White background, navy text, gold accents
- Mobile-first responsive layout

## 8. Vial Label Standards

### Format
- Black-and-white only (Niimbot B1 thermal printer)
- All-black emblem (no fill color)
- Sans-serif type (Inter or Helvetica)

### Required elements
- Lot number (top-left): `Lot: RT251222`
- Reconstitution date when applicable (above lot): `Recon: 02/01/26` (MM/DD/YY)
- "For Research Use Only" (left side, italic or regular)
- Emblem (center)
- Product name below emblem (bold, sans-serif)
- Dosage (top-right, white-on-black box): `24mg`
- Form label below dosage: `Lyophilized` or conversion ratio (`10u = 1mg`)

### Date format
Always MM/DD/YY. Never spell out the month. Never use slashes inconsistently.

## 9. Customer-Facing Copy Conventions

### Pricing
Never quote prices in chat, email, or printed materials. Always: "Check the site for current pricing."

### Stock levels
Never quote real-time stock. Always: "If it's listed on the site, it's available."

### Shipping
Don't promise specific delivery windows. General language only: "We ship Monday through Wednesday during warm months to protect cold-chain."

### Membership messaging
Frame membership around quality control and manageable scale. Never explain that it's about payment processor compliance.

> **Example:** "We keep our member base small so we can stay accountable to every order."
>
> **Not:** "Membership lets us protect our payment processing setup from competitors."

### Payment flow messaging
Lead with the benefit (security, privacy) when explaining the Square invoice workflow.

> **Example:** "You'll get a secure payment link by text and email within about a minute. This keeps your card details off our store and processed by a separate, PCI-compliant system."
>
> **Not:** "We can't accept payment at checkout because our processor doesn't allow it for our category."

### Research use only
Always present in product pages, policies, and packaging. Never apologize for it, never wink at it, never explain its real purpose. It is the framework. Move on.

## 10. AI Chat Agent Rules

The agent (deployed via GHL on the Shopify store) follows these hard rules:

1. **No human persona.** Signs as "Everform Life," not a fake name.
2. **Transparent about being AI** if directly asked. Doesn't volunteer it unprompted.
3. **Cannot recommend products in response to health goals.** This includes the loophole "I can't recommend X but we carry Y which is studied for Z." That's still a recommendation. Closed.
4. **No dosing or protocol advice.** Ever. Direct people to pep-pedia.org if pushed.
5. **No pricing in chat.** Direct to the site.
6. **No human use discussion.** Deflect without lecturing.

### Acceptable redirect language

> "I can tell you what's in the vial and how to store it, but I can't go beyond that."
>
> "Can't advise on that one. Happy to help with product or order questions though."
>
> "Check the site for current pricing."

## 11. Partner & Referral Program

- Commission rates are **not published publicly**. Don't list them on the partner page or in any marketing copy.
- Partner page lives in the footer, not the main nav.
- Referral codes auto-approve applicants from verified partners. Organic signups go to manual review.
- "How did you find us?" is a required application question with an optional referring member name field.

## 12. Legal & Compliance Posture

Every customer-facing surface (website, packaging, email, chat, partner pages) must:

- Carry "Research Use Only" framing
- Avoid claims about human use, dosing, or therapeutic effect
- Avoid medical, clinical, or pharmaceutical language ("treats," "cures," "prescribes")
- Keep the LLC name and physical address off public-facing pages
- Use Locksmith for cart/checkout gating
- Surface RUA acceptance at Shopify checkout
